# Privacy Policy – Escrovana Market Discord Bot

**Last Updated:** October 12, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Escrovana Market** Discord bot (“the Bot”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with it.

Escrovana Market is an OTC (over-the-counter) marketplace bot that lets users create buy/sell listings, earn and spend points, join giveaways, and receive rewards for engaging with official X (Twitter) posts.

By using the Bot, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to operate the Bot’s features:

- **Discord User ID** and username / display name
- **Server (Guild) ID** and Channel IDs where the bot is used
- **Listing content** you create (title, description, price, contact info you provide)
- **Points balance** and related transaction history
- **Giveaway entries** (user IDs of participants)
- **Command usage** related to listings, points, and giveaways
- **X (Twitter) engagement data** you voluntarily submit for point rewards (post links, verification proofs)

We do **not** collect:
- Passwords
- Email addresses or phone numbers (unless you explicitly provide them in a listing)
- Full message history of the server
- Presence / online status
- Payment card details

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data solely to:
- Display and manage buy/sell listings
- Track and award points
- Run giveaways and select winners
- Verify X post engagement for point rewards
- Assign role rewards based on points
- Moderate listings and prevent spam/abuse
- Provide support when you contact us

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising.

## 3. Data Storage & Retention

- Data is stored in a secure MongoDB database.
- Listing and points data is retained while the feature is active and for a reasonable period afterward for audit/support purposes.
- You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 5).
- We delete or anonymize data that is no longer needed.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not share personal data except:
- When required by law
- With Discord (as the platform the bot runs on)
- With service providers necessary to operate the bot (e.g., hosting)

## 5. Your Rights

You can:
- Request a copy of the data we store about you
- Request correction or deletion of your data
- Opt out of certain features

To make a request, open a support ticket in the official Escrovana Discord server or contact the bot developer via the support channel.

## 6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last Updated” date will be revised. Continued use of the Bot after changes means you accept the updated policy.

## 8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data requests, please open a ticket in the official Escrovana Discord server or contact the bot administrators through the support system.
